🏡 About Adult Family Homes

An Adult Family Home is a licensed residential home providing personal care, meals, supervision, and assistance to adults who need help with daily living. Washington State AFHs house up to 6 residents and are regulated by DSHS, offering a warm, home-like alternative to larger nursing facilities.
Adult Family Homes offer a significantly smaller, more personalized environment than nursing homes or large assisted living facilities. With fewer residents, caregivers can dedicate more individual attention to each person, leading to better outcomes, deeper relationships, and a true home atmosphere.
Yes. Sunrise Adult Family Home LLC is fully licensed by the Washington State Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS). We comply with all state regulations and undergo regular inspections to maintain the highest standards of care.

📋 Admissions & Visiting

Scheduling a tour is easy. You can:
  • Use our online tour request form
  • Call us directly at (206) 557-2149
  • Email us at frity1995@yahoo.com
Tours are available 7 days a week by appointment. We look forward to welcoming you.
Our thoughtful admission process has five steps:
  • Initial consultation to understand your loved one's needs
  • Personal home tour at your convenience
  • Comprehensive care assessment
  • Resident agreement review and signing
  • Personalized care plan development
Our team supports families through every step of the journey, ensuring a smooth and stress-free transition.
We warmly welcome family visits and strongly encourage family involvement in residents' lives — it makes a meaningful difference in wellbeing and happiness. We kindly ask that visits be arranged in advance to ensure the best possible experience for your loved one and all residents in our home.
To help your loved one feel right at home from day one, please bring:
  • Personal clothing and items for the season
  • Favorite photos, personal décor, and comfort items
  • Medical records and insurance information
  • Current medications list and prescriptions
We provide a comprehensive checklist during the admission process so nothing is overlooked.
