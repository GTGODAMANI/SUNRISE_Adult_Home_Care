A Family Home, Not a Facility

Sunrise Adult Family Home LLC is a state-licensed residential care home, proudly serving up to six residents at a time. Unlike large institutional facilities, we maintain an intentionally small household — ensuring every resident receives the individual attention, warmth, and familiarity that only a true home can offer.

Our warm, residential setting on 7902 58th PL NE in Marysville, Washington, was carefully chosen and thoughtfully designed to feel like a real home. From our welcoming living spaces to our peaceful outdoor garden, every detail reflects our deep commitment to comfort, beauty, and belonging.

We were founded with a clear purpose: to create a genuine home-like alternative to large nursing facilities where residents can thrive in a safe, loving environment. We believe that quality senior care is not just about medical support — it's about laughter, connection, purpose, and the simple joys of everyday life. That belief guides everything we do at Sunrise.
