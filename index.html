Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions families ask about our care home.

An Adult Family Home (AFH) is a licensed residential home that provides personal care, meals, and supervision to up to six adults who need assistance with daily activities. Unlike large nursing facilities, AFHs offer a small, home-like environment with personalized, individualized care. In Washington State, all AFHs are regulated by the Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS).
Our monthly care fee typically includes room and board, all meals and snacks, personal care assistance, medication management, housekeeping and laundry, and 24/7 supervision. Additional specialized services may be arranged based on individual care needs. We encourage families to contact us directly to discuss specific care requirements and our current fee structure.
