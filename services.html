Our Care Services

Comprehensive, personalized care services designed to support your loved one's health, independence, and dignity.

What We Provide

A Full Spectrum of Compassionate Care

At Sunrise Adult Family Home, every service we provide is rooted in a person-centered philosophy. We work closely with each resident and their family to develop individualized care plans that honor personal preferences, medical needs, and lifestyle goals. Our goal is simple: to help your loved one thrive in a safe, warm, home-like environment.

  • ✓ Licensed & Regulated
  • ✓ Personalized Care Plans
  • ✓ 24/7 Caregiver Support
  • ✓ Family Communication
  • ✓ Skilled, Trained Staff
  • ✓ Home-Like Environment
Our Services in Detail

Everything Your Loved One Needs

🛁

Personal Care Assistance

We provide dignified, respectful assistance with all aspects of personal care including bathing, showering, dressing, grooming, oral hygiene, and toileting. Our caregivers are trained to preserve dignity and promote independence wherever possible, ensuring every resident feels comfortable, respected, and cared for throughout their daily routine.

What's Included

  • ✓ Bathing and showering assistance
  • ✓ Dressing and undressing help
  • ✓ Grooming and personal hygiene
  • ✓ Toileting and incontinence care
  • ✓ Mobility and transfer support
  • ✓ Skin care and pressure prevention
💊

Medication Management

Safe and accurate medication management is a cornerstone of quality care. Our trained caregivers ensure all medications are administered correctly and on schedule, meticulously tracking dosages and maintaining clear documentation. We maintain open lines of communication with pharmacies and healthcare providers to ensure your loved one's medical needs are always met safely and effectively.

What's Included

  • ✓ Medication reminders and prompting
  • ✓ Medication administration
  • ✓ Dosage tracking and documentation
  • ✓ Coordination with healthcare providers
  • ✓ Pharmacy communication
  • ✓ Adverse reaction monitoring
🏡

Daily Living Support

We are committed to maintaining a clean, comfortable, and welcoming home environment for all our residents. From preparing nutritious, home-cooked meals tailored to dietary needs, to keeping the home tidy and managing laundry, our caregivers handle the everyday tasks that help create a joyful and comfortable daily life for every resident.

What's Included

  • ✓ Home-cooked nutritious meals
  • ✓ Housekeeping and tidying
  • ✓ Laundry and linen care
  • ✓ Grocery coordination
  • ✓ Activity assistance
  • ✓ Personal scheduling support
🧠

Memory Care Support

Our specialized memory care program is designed to support individuals living with Alzheimer's disease, dementia, and other memory-related conditions. We create a safe, familiar, and structured environment that reduces confusion and promotes calm. Our caregivers are trained in dementia-specific best practices, using compassionate techniques to support residents and provide invaluable guidance to families throughout the care journey.

What's Included

  • ✓ Alzheimer's and dementia care
  • ✓ Safe supervised environment
  • ✓ Routine-based daily schedules
  • ✓ Behavioral support
  • ✓ Family guidance and education
  • ✓ Sensory engagement activities
🌿

Respite Care

Caring for a loved one is deeply rewarding, but every family caregiver deserves a break. Our respite care program offers short-term residential stays that provide family caregivers with the rest they need while ensuring their loved one continues to receive the same high-quality, attentive care. Flexible scheduling options are available to accommodate your family's needs, including vacation and recovery support.

What's Included

  • ✓ Short-term residential stays
  • ✓ Full care services included
  • ✓ Flexible scheduling
  • ✓ Vacation respite coverage
  • ✓ Medical recovery support
  • ✓ Trial period option
🛡️

24-Hour Supervision

The safety and well-being of our residents is our highest priority. Our caregivers are present around the clock — day and night — to monitor each resident's health, respond swiftly to any emergency, and perform regular safety assessments. Comprehensive fall prevention protocols and proactive health monitoring ensure every resident remains safe, comfortable, and supported at all times.

What's Included

  • ✓ Round-the-clock caregiver presence
  • ✓ Nighttime monitoring
  • ✓ Emergency response protocols
  • ✓ Fall prevention measures
  • ✓ Health monitoring
  • ✓ Safety checks and assessments
