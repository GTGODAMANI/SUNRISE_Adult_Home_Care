Common Questions

Tour FAQs

💰

Is there a cost for the tour?

No, tours are completely free with absolutely no obligation. We want you to feel comfortable exploring your options without any pressure.

👨‍👩‍👧

Can I bring family members?

Absolutely — we encourage family members to attend! Making this decision as a family is important, and we welcome everyone who wants to be part of the conversation.

⏱️

How long does a tour take?

Typically 30–60 minutes depending on your questions and how much detail you'd like. We never rush — take as long as you need.
