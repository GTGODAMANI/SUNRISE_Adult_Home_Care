Privacy Policy

How Sunrise Adult Family Home LLC collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Effective Date January 1, 2025
Last Updated January 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to the website of Sunrise Adult Family Home LLC ("Sunrise AFH," "we," "us," or "our"), a licensed adult family home located at 7902 58th PL NE, Marysville, WA 98270. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of everyone who visits our website or contacts us to inquire about our services.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information you provide when you visit our website at sunriseafh.com (the "Site") or interact with us through our contact forms. Please read this policy carefully. By using our Site, you agree to the practices described in this document.

If you have questions or concerns about this policy or our data practices, you may contact us directly at any time using the information provided in Section 9 below.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us when you use our website. This may include:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, and email address submitted through our contact or tour request forms.
  • Care Inquiry Information: Details about your loved one's care needs, such as the type of care required (e.g., memory care, personal care, respite care) and the name of the prospective resident.
  • Scheduling Information: Preferred tour dates and times you request.
  • Communications: Any messages, questions, or notes you include in form submissions or emails.
  • Referral Source: Optional information about how you found our website (e.g., Google, referral, social media).

We also automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information when you visit our Site, including browser type, referring URLs, pages viewed, and time spent on pages. This information is collected through standard web server logs and analytics tools and cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • Responding to Inquiries: To respond to questions, requests, or concerns you submit through our website or by phone and email.
  • Scheduling Tours: To confirm and schedule in-person tours of our home at your convenience.
  • Discussing Care Options: To facilitate conversations about care services appropriate for your loved one's needs.
  • Improving Our Services: To understand how visitors use our website and identify areas for improvement in our online presence and service delivery.
  • Compliance: To comply with applicable legal obligations as required by Washington State law and federal regulations governing adult family homes.

We will never use your information for unsolicited marketing purposes, and we will not contact you in ways you have not consented to.

4. Information Sharing

We respect your privacy deeply and do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing or commercial purposes.

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use Formspree (formspree.io) as our form submission processing service. Information submitted through our contact forms is transmitted through Formspree's secure infrastructure before being delivered to us. Formspree is bound by their own privacy policy and does not use your data for independent purposes.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or government authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sunrise AFH, our residents, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the unlikely event that Sunrise AFH undergoes a change of ownership or business transfer, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to continued protection consistent with this policy.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

All form submissions are processed through HTTPS-encrypted connections, and we use reputable third-party form services with their own security standards. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Inquiry information is typically retained for a reasonable period to allow follow-up communications.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to support basic website functionality and improve your browsing experience. These may include:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled without affecting website performance.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous usage data. We may use tools such as Google Analytics for this purpose.

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of our website. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites or for advertising purposes.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services that may collect or process information:

  • Formspree (formspree.io): We use Formspree to process form submissions from our contact and tour request forms. When you submit a form, your data is transmitted through Formspree's servers to our email. Please review Formspree's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle data.
  • Google Fonts: We may load fonts from Google Fonts, which may set a cookie or collect your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Google Analytics (if applicable): We may use Google Analytics to understand website traffic patterns. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data. You may opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services and encourage you to review their policies independently.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. These may include:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information we have on file.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information from our records, subject to any legal retention obligations.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may opt out of receiving any future communications from us by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 9. We will respond to all reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

Washington State residents may have additional rights under applicable state privacy laws. We are committed to honoring those rights as required.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information. Your continued use of our website following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

If any changes significantly affect how we handle your personal information, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you, such as by posting a prominent notice on our website.

Thank you for trusting Sunrise Adult Family Home LLC. Protecting your privacy is an integral part of the respectful, dignified care we provide to every individual and family we serve.
