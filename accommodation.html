Our Spaces

Comfortable Living Spaces

Our home at 7902 58th PL NE, Marysville offers 5 private bedrooms in a warm, residential setting — a true home, not a facility.

Every detail of our home has been thoughtfully curated to foster comfort, safety, and dignity. From warm lighting and soft furnishings to accessible bathrooms and wide hallways, our physical environment supports both independence and wellbeing.

We believe that where you live shapes how you feel. That's why residents are encouraged to personalize their private bedrooms with familiar items, family photos, and cherished mementos. Your space is truly yours.

Shared spaces including the living room, dining area, and outdoor garden are maintained to the highest standards of cleanliness and comfort, offering welcoming places to relax, socialize, and enjoy daily life with fellow residents and visiting family members.
